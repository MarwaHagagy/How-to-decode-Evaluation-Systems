# How to Decode Evaluation Systems

> **Interviews are not interrogations. ** They are measurement instruments.**

Let me begin by saying, I hate interviews. I dreaded ever being asked to do one, and I am the most talkative person you will have the pleasure of meeting in your life. But the feeling of being judged by what you say is terrifying. 

That was until I started learning qualitative research methods and survey design. I realized that there is an actual science behind interview questions. They are not a random collection of questions designed to intimidate you; they are a highly specific measurement instrument.

People always tell us, "When you are presented with X question, answer using Y and Z." Instead, this handbook discusses *what each question was designed to test*. When you understand the underlying process, you can answer as you see fit with your own words. No more memorizing "best answers."
